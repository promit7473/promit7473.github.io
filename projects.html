Manipulator Kinematics and Dynamics from Scratch

From-scratch kinematics and dynamics in NumPy for two arms — a 6-DOF UR5e and 7-DOF KUKA iiwa — validated against official URDFs to 1.5e-7. Caught a sign error in the UR5e DH table that left the model 1.998 m off. Numerical IK with damped least squares, Cartesian paths via quintic blending and quaternion slerp. No MoveIt, KDL, or Pinocchio.

RRT* Motion Planning in 7-DOF Configuration Space

Asymptotically optimal sampling-based motion planning for a KUKA iiwa, implemented from first principles in NumPy. RRT* searches the full 7-D joint space with real URDF collision checking, converging to within 0.16% of the analytic optimum in 2D validation. The scene includes a table, wall obstacle, and mesh objects (mug, Lego, Jenga). Greedy shortcutting cuts path cost by 28%.
